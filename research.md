# Research

We combine methods from computational linguistics, machine learning and
experimental psycholinguistics to understand human language and the systems
that model it.

## Computational models of language learning

Placeholder: how children and machines acquire language from linguistic and
perceptual input, and what computational models can tell us about the
learning problem.

## Human language processing

Placeholder: experimental and modelling work on how people comprehend and
produce language in real time, using methods such as reading times,
eye-tracking and EEG.

## Understanding neural language models

Placeholder: analysing and interpreting the representations learned by neural
models of text and speech, and comparing them to human processing.
