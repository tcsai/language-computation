# Computational Linguistics & Psycholinguistics

We are a research unit of the
[Research Center for Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/about/schools/tshd/departments/dca)
at Tilburg University. We build formal and computational models of human
language acquisition and use, and use what we learn about human language
processing to develop better language technology.

Read about our [research](research.md), meet the [people](people.md), or see
the latest [news](news.md).
